AlphaWolves

Brand identity and go-to-market plan for a trading-education and community brand launching in Indonesia: a full logo family plus the plan for how the brand finds, engages and converts its audience.

Year2022
MarketIndonesia
IndustryFintech / trading community
ScopeIdentity & go-to-market
Brand identityGo-to-marketSocial & contentLaunch plan
AlphaWolves

One wolf, five packs

A master brand and four sub-brands for different product lines. They share one visual language: dynamic wolf marks in crimson and teal, and a clean rounded wordmark. The wolf theme carries into the naming of every membership tier.

AlphaWolves

The funnel

A four-stage acquisition path, so every channel has a clear job: from first view to signed client.

01

Attract

Meta & Instagram ads, LinkedIn ads, influencers and TikTok Live to pull in first-time visitors.

02

Capture

Turn attention into contacts: social Q&A, customer-relations follow-up and small in-person gatherings.

03

Engage

Free trials, a free community group, webinars and workshops, plus a referral community.

04

Convert

A tailored proposal for each warm lead, with every ad and channel funnelled into one website.

AlphaWolves

Channel strategy

Live video at the centre, with every other channel fed by it, so one live session becomes a week of content.

TikTok Live

The content engine: live sessions at least twice a week mixing market education with live Q&A. Every live is clipped into short-form content for the other channels.

Instagram

Content pillars: weekly market analysis, education, community recaps, testimonials and product explainers, built on an existing account instead of starting from zero.

YouTube

Every TikTok Live is simulcast to YouTube, alongside explainer videos that answer the questions the community keeps asking.

LinkedIn

A channel competitors in the market were ignoring, used to reach a more professional audience outside the usual social feeds.

Paid ads

Ads funnel into a website with live chat and contact forms. Creatives are cut from live streams in a vlog or podcast style that feels native rather than salesy.

Referral programme

A three-level referral structure that rewards members and partners for bringing in new members, turning the community into a growth channel.

AlphaWolves

Launch roadmap

Three phases from foundation to growth, with a 9-day sprint before the soft launch.

Phase 1 — Foundation

Partnership MoU, website live, graphic designer hired, ad creative designed

Phase 2 — Partnerships

Platform partnerships and account setup; first products prepared for launch

Phase 3 — Launch

Social feeds refreshed, content calendar running, ads live, micro-influencers and a micro-agency onboarded

9-day sprint

Designer and admin hiring, payment forms, website trial, first content upload and partner onboarding, ending in soft launch
